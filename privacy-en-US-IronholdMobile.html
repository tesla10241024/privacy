Scope and publisher

This policy describes the installed iPhone and iPad game Prism Front.

Publisher / data controller: REQUIRED — enter the actual developer or company name.

Offline play and data

The game works offline. It has no accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking, cloud services or multiplayer. The developer does not collect gameplay data or personal data through the game, and the game does not transmit such data to the developer or third parties.

Levels, artwork, code and other game resources are included in the app. Mission state is processed on the device. The game uses the device’s preferred languages to choose its interface language; this preference is not sent to the developer. No account or remote profile is created. The pause-menu Privacy button displays a local privacy notice in each of the 24 supported interface languages.

Permissions and device features

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or Bluetooth, and does not use advertising identifiers. Game sounds are generated locally; they do not require microphone access. A virtual camera renders the 3D scene and does not use the physical camera.

Retention and your choices

The developer has no server-side gameplay or personal records to retain, sell, share or delete. You can close or remove the app using your device’s controls. This statement does not describe Apple’s own App Store, operating-system or diagnostic settings.

Support and this website

If you choose to contact support after a real contact method is published, the information you voluntarily send will leave your device. The publisher must specify how those messages are handled before opening that channel. The game itself has no support form.

This static page has no analytics or cookies. A future hosting provider may process ordinary web connection logs; the selected provider and any related disclosure must be reviewed before publication.
