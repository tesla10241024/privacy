# Privacy review — local candidate build

The game stores selected hero/island, difficulty, interface language, frame-rate limit, best results and completed missions on the device. No account creation, ads, analytics, authentication, remote AI or network gameplay is implemented. Fonts and models are bundled. No microphone, camera, photos or location permission is requested by game code; empty usage descriptions were removed from the iOS wrapper.

Godot runtime and the existing privacy manifest remain bundled. App Store privacy answers must be checked against the final linked archive before submission. No store submission or account settings were changed during this work.
